About Smart Cleanliness Reporting System

This platform provides a simple way for students to report cleanliness issues around campus. Instead of waiting for someone else to notice a problem, students can quickly report an issue by selecting its location, describing the problem and uploading a photo.

The system converts everyday observations into useful data that can help administrators identify problem areas and respond more efficiently.

Why This System?

  • Faster reporting
  • Better monitoring
  • Data-driven cleanliness management
  • Student participation
  • Improved campus environment
